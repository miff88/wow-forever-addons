# Proc Plates is now part of Proc City

**Proc Plates (formerly Overpower Plates) has moved into [Proc City](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/proc-city).** Everything it did is there now, alongside your other procs and buffs, and this page won't get any more updates.

## What to do
1. Install **[Proc City](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/proc-city)** from CurseForge.
2. Log in once with both installed. Proc City brings your Proc Plates settings over, and Proc Plates steps aside so you never get doubled icons.
3. Delete Proc Plates (the `OverpowerPlates` folder) whenever you like.

## What you get in Proc City
- **Overpower on the right mob:** the icon above the nameplate of the mob that dodged you, including Whirlwind and Cleave dodges on mobs you aren't targeting. Dodges against other players are ignored.
- **Revenge, Riposte, Counterattack and Mongoose Bite:** in your proc row for their 5 second window, only while the game says you can cast them.
- **Button glow** on your action bars for all of them, plus Shadow Bolt during Shadow Trance.
- **All your other procs and short buffs** in the same row: Flurry, Enrage, Crusader, Clearcasting, trinkets and more, with pinning to keep the important ones on screen.

The last Proc Plates version (2.1.0) still works on its own if you'd rather not switch yet, and reminds you at login that it has moved.

## More WoW Forever addons
- **[Proc City](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/proc-city)**: your procs and short buffs as a row of icons, including the new Forever talents, plus Overpower on the nameplate of the mob that dodged.
- **[Mage Services](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/mage-services)**: catches portal, food and water requests in chat, with one-click invite, whisper and portal.
- **[Buff Notice](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/battle-shout-notice)**: an icon when a buff your class keeps up is missing or about to run out.
- **[Kick Tally](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/kick-tally)**: announces your successful interrupts and counts your group's kicks.
- **[Kill Clock](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/kill-clock)**: how long until your target dies, and until it reaches execute range.
- **[Plate Threat](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/plate-threat)**: your threat on every enemy nameplate, with a warning before you pull aggro.
- **[Target Arrow](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/target-arrow)**: an arrow over your target so you can find it in any crowd.
