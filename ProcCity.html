Proc City

Never miss the moment. Proc City shows the abilities that light up for a few seconds in a fight, as a movable row of icons with the time left on each, and lights their buttons on your action bars. Overpower also marks the mob that dodged you.

Made for World of Warcraft: Forever. For warriors, rogues and hunters.

Features

  • Warriors: Overpower after a mob dodges you, Revenge after you block, dodge or parry, and Victory Rush after a kill.
  • Rogues: Riposte after you parry. Hunters: Counterattack after you parry, and Mongoose Bite after your target dodges.
  • Overpower on the right mob: an Overpower icon sits above the nameplate of the mob that dodged you, including Whirlwind and Cleave dodges on mobs you aren't targeting. Dodges against other players are ignored. A charge with no dodge to match (Bloodthrill, or a mob without a nameplate) still shows in the row.
  • Button glow: their buttons light up while they're usable, on Blizzard bars, Bartender4, ElvUI and Dominos.
  • They only show while the game says you can cast them, and go away once you use them.
  • Pinned: pin the ones you build around and they keep their place, shown as a faint grey icon while they're down. Warriors get Overpower pinned the first time they log in.
  • Icons grow to the right or left, with the seconds left, an optional clock sweep and an optional sound. Drag it anywhere; test mode shows sample icons.
  • Coming from Proc Plates or Overpower Plates? Proc City brings your settings over, and Proc Plates steps aside once Proc City is installed.

Commands

Command What it does
/proc Open settings
/proc test Show test icons
/proc move / /proc lock Move the icons
/proc grow left or right Which way the icons grow
/proc list What's shown for your class
/proc off <name> / /proc on <name> Hide or show one
/proc pin <name> / /proc unpin <name> Keep it on screen, greyed out while it's down
/proc glow Turn the action button glow on or off
/proc plates Turn the Overpower nameplate icon on or off (warriors)
/proc debug What Proc City sees

Buff procs (Flurry, Crusader, Clearcasting...)

WoW Forever hides your buffs from addons in combat, so no addon can show buff procs mid-fight. Proc City 1.x tried, and could only catch up once combat ended. For those, use the game's own Cooldown Manager: turn it on under Options > Gameplay > Advanced, then add the buffs you want to its Tracked Buffs.

More WoW Forever addons

  • Mage Services: catches portal, food and water requests in chat, with one-click invite, whisper and portal.
  • Buff Notice: an icon when a buff your class keeps up is missing or about to run out.
  • Kick Tally: announces your successful interrupts and counts your group's kicks.
  • Kill Clock: how long until your target dies, and until it reaches execute range.
  • Plate Threat: your threat on every enemy nameplate, with a warning before you pull aggro.
  • Target Arrow: an arrow over your target so you can find it in any crowd.
